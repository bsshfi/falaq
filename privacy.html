📍موقعك

  • يطلب التطبيق موقعك من الجوال (GPS) أو تختار مدينتك بنفسك، ليحسب المواقيت والقبلة.
  • الحساب كله يتم داخل جهازك، والموقع يُحفظ في ذاكرة المتصفح على جهازك فقط.
  • لا يُرسل الموقع إلينا إلا إذا فعّلت الإشعارات، وبالتقريب المذكور أدناه.

🧭البوصلة

📱البيانات المحفوظة على جهازك

تُحفظ في ذاكرة المتصفح على جهازك فقط: إعداداتك (اللغة والمظهر وطريقة الحساب والتعديلات)، وموقعك أو مدينتك، واختياراتك للإشعارات، وتقدّمك في الأذكار. لا نستطيع نحن الاطلاع عليها، وتُحذف بإزالة التطبيق أو مسح بيانات المتصفح.

🔔الإشعارات (اختيارية)

إذا فعّلت الإشعارات، يُرسل التطبيق إلى خادم الإشعارات الخاص بنا (على Cloudflare) ما يلزم لإرسالها في وقتها فقط:

  • عنوان الاشتراك الذي يولّده متصفحك للإشعارات.
  • موقعك مقرّباً لكيلومتر تقريباً، ومنطقتك الزمنية ولغة التطبيق.

👋Who we are

FALAQ (فلق) is an app by WAMD ARC for prayer times, the qibla direction, the moon and its mansions, the Najdi stars and daily adhkar. It runs in the browser at falaq.wamdarc.com and can be added to your Home Screen. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

📍Your location

  • The app asks for your phone’s location (GPS), or you pick your city yourself, to calculate prayer times and the qibla.
  • All calculations run on your device, and the location is stored in your browser’s storage on your device only.
  • Your location is sent to us only if you turn on notifications, and only approximately, as described below.

🧭Compass

When you start the compass, the app reads your phone’s orientation sensors live to show the qibla. These readings are never stored or sent.

📱Data stored on your device

Stored in your browser on your device only: your settings (language, theme, calculation method and adjustments), your location or city, your notification choices, and your adhkar progress. We cannot see this data, and it is deleted when you remove the app or clear your browser data.

🔔Notifications (optional)

If you turn on notifications, the app sends our notification server (on Cloudflare) only what is needed to deliver them on time:

  • The push subscription address your browser creates.
  • Your location rounded to about one kilometer, your time zone and app language.
  • Your calculation method, Asr method, minute adjustments, and the prayers and reminders you chose.

Your name and any identifying details are never sent. When you turn notifications off, your subscription is deleted from the server.

📊Anonymous usage statistics

To know how many people use our apps, the app sends on opening: the app name, a random ID generated on your device, whether the app is installed on the Home Screen, the device type, the version number and the language. We do not use cookies, and your location is never part of these statistics.

💬In-app messages

The app may show a short message from our team, such as news about an update. To fetch it, the app asks our server for the current message without sending any data about you.

🌐Other services

  • The app is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log your IP address in its usual server logs.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may let Google see your IP address.
  • No external maps or prayer-time provider is used; all calculations are astronomical, inside the app.

🧒Children

The app is family friendly, needs no account, and collects no personal data from any user, including children.

🗑️Deleting your data

  • Turn off notifications in Settings to delete your subscription and approximate location from our server.
  • Delete everything by removing the app from your Home Screen or clearing the site’s data in your browser settings.

📝Changes

We may update this policy when we add features, and the “Last updated” date at the top will change.
